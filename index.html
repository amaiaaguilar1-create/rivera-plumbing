What to call about

Say which of these is happening. If you are not sure, describe the water.

Leaks

Supply lines, valves, and pipes that are dripping or running.

Drains

Sinks, tubs, and toilets that will not clear.

Water heaters

No hot water, or a tank that is leaking.

Fixtures

Faucets, toilets, and sinks that need repair.

Before you dial

Shut the valve

If water is spreading, turn the valve under the fixture, or the main valve if you can reach it.

Note the spot

See which room, which fixture, and whether the water is hot or cold.

Call and describe it

Tell us what you see. We will say what happens next.
